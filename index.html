LIGHTBOX STUDIO

A place for stories to become films.

Lightbox Studio is a browser-based filmmaking and screenwriting workspace. Write screenplays. Develop story foundations and characters. Organize places, visual references and the scenes that connect them. Lightbox Studio brings the creative materials behind a film into one browser-based workspace.

Two ways to use Lightbox. Continue in your browser without installing anything, or download the Android APK to get the app and its home-screen widgets. Download Lightbox Studio 1.0.7 for Android.
