# Lightbox visual system

## Scene and intention
A writer moves between a laptop at a daylight desk and a phone between locations, collecting ideas before returning to long, focused writing sessions. Use a light paper work surface with a dark ink navigation rail, never a dark screenplay page.

## Colour strategy
A restrained writing room opens into a full palette for the atlas: apricot for the spark, mint for research, lavender for visual direction, blue for world profiles. Vermilion identifies the primary action. Colour accompanies labels, never replaces them. Tokens use OKLCH in app/studio-refresh.css.

## Typography
DM Sans carries controls; Instrument Serif carries story headings and seed writing; DM Mono carries compact metadata. Courier Prime and screenplay dimensions remain dedicated to screenplay pages. Body text stays within readable line lengths.

## Layout and interaction
One document row and one compact toolbar in the screenplay. Formatting exceptions live in More. Chapter navigation replaces the foundation questionnaire; profile sections separate dramatic choices, history, visual references, connections and free notes. Existing saved fields retain their keys.

## Motion
A 200ms entrance on changing development chapters or profile sections signals navigation. Atlas hover movement is 2px. No moving text while composing. Disable both effects for reduced motion.
