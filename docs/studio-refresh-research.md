# Lightbox studio workflow research

## Screenplay: make typing the primary interaction
[Fountain syntax](https://fountain.io/syntax/) defines scene headings from INT./EXT., uppercase character cues and dialogue after a character. Its explicit prefixes handle exceptions such as uppercase action. Lightbox already had most of this detection; the visible controls obscured it. Move paragraph overrides into More, remove the duplicate phone Dialogue button, preserve undo and emphasis, and provide a typing guide. Keep shouted dialogue as dialogue and retain formatting in saved documents.

## Story foundation: an editable model, not a survey
[Final Draft's Beat Board](https://kb.finaldraft.com/hc/en-us/articles/27608580196244-What-is-the-Beat-Board) supports flexible notes, scenes and sequences. Its [logline and beats guide](https://www.finaldraft.com/learn/loglines-story-beats-brainstorming/) connects story planning to a working outline. [Pixar in a Box's character vocabulary](https://www.khanacademy.org/computing/pixar/storytelling/character/a/character-glossary) distinguishes desire, need, obstacles, stakes and change.

Implementation: five chapters retain the original questions but reveal them in context. A live sentence joins protagonist, pursuit, pressure and stakes. Turning points store title and consequence, support ordering, and can become screenplay scenes. Open questions and free exploration remain optional. No scoring of artistic quality or forced formula.

## Profiles: dramatic purpose, visual identity and connections
[Milanote's character guide](https://milanote.com/guide/characters) combines visual references with goals, personality and backstory. Its [film moodboard guide](https://milanote.com/guide/film-moodboard) gathers visual direction in one place. [StudioBinder's element reports](https://support.studiobinder.com/en/articles/11166483-how-to-view-element-list-reports) show where props and production elements recur across scenes.

Implementation: character desire and resistance sit beside need, stakes and voice. History and change receive their own workspace. Object profiles separate dramatic purpose, recognisable features, ownership, condition and practical requirements. Existing image upload, captions and cover ordering remain available in a labelled Images section. Connections link to other profiles and reveal active-draft scene appearances detected by the existing profile-linking system. Detection is not an exhaustive continuity guarantee.

## Colour, hierarchy and motion
[Adobe's colour contrast guide](https://www.adobe.com/uk/creativecloud/design/discover/color-contrast.html) treats colour alongside size, spacing and position. [WCAG contrast guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/contrast-minimum) requires 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text. [W3C interaction animation guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions) supports disabling nonessential motion.

Design inference: warm paper provides a clear writing surface; ink navigation separates chrome from content. Apricot, mint, lavender and blue give the creative rooms distinct identities. Vermilion marks actions. Brief section changes and hover feedback make navigation responsive without adding a moving-text distraction to the editor. Reduced motion is respected.
