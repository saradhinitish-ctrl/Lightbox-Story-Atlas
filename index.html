LIGHTBOX STUDIO

A place for stories to become films.

Lightbox Studio is a browser-based filmmaking and screenwriting workspace created by Bitra Nitish Saradhi. Write screenplays. Develop story foundations and characters. Organize places, visual references and the scenes that connect them. Lightbox Studio brings the creative materials behind a film into one browser-based workspace.
