About the workspace

Give every story a place to become a film.

Lightbox Studio is a browser-based creative workspace for filmmakers and screenwriters. It brings story development, screenplay writing, visual references and pre-production planning into one environment.

Open LightboxMeet the creator

What it is

Built around the filmmaking process.

Developing a film involves more than producing a script. Writers and directors collect ideas, develop characters, research places, organize scenes and plan how a story should look and feel on screen.

Lightbox aims to connect these elements in one workspace, from early exploration to more structured preparation.
