The creative toolkit

Six reasons to look closer.

01

A screenplay that travels

Import and export Fountain and Final Draft (FDX), compile screenplay PDFs, and work with screenplay-specific formatting.

02

Profiles that connect to the page

Use @Name references to link character, location and prop profiles in screenplay text and moodboard notes, and open the linked profiles.

03

Visual direction for individual scenes

Work with scene boards, film-wide direction, colour studies, image references, lighting ideas and camera or rhythm notes.

04

A beat timeline with dramatic intention

Plan emotion, tension, pressure, cast and approximate minutes alongside screenplay scenes.

05

Draft versions without losing your world

Create new screenplay draft versions while keeping characters, locations and research shared in the project.

06

From scene to director's view

Move between Story Atlas, scene breakdown and director pitch views to explore scripts, images and scene assets.

A connected workflow

From writing a scene to seeing the film.

Write a scene, refer to character or location profiles using @Name, gather references in its visual board, explore its tension and emotional progression in the beat timeline, then prepare another draft without leaving the existing project world behind.

For exchanging screenplay files, Lightbox includes Fountain and Final Draft (FDX) tools alongside screenplay PDF export.
