# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Writers and filmmakers developing screenplays and manuscripts, collecting characters, locations, research and visual references in one project.

## Product Purpose
Lightbox connects writing with the story materials behind a film. The first-run flow should make choosing a format and creating a world clear and effortless.

## Brand Personality
Calm, considered, creative. Preserve the existing warm studio atmosphere.

## Anti-references
Layered panels that look accidental, detached action buttons, decorative clutter and generic questionnaires.

## Design Principles
- Make the next action clear.
- Keep related controls visually connected.
- Preserve writing focus and existing workflows.
- Treat small screens as complete workspaces.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Maintain keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable text and comfortable touch targets.
