One project, connected thinking

Write the scene. Develop its world. Consider its image.

In filmmaking, writing, character research and visual decisions often influence one another. Lightbox gives these different stages a shared creative home rather than treating them as unrelated documents.

The goal is to make movement between story development, drafting and visual planning feel natural.
